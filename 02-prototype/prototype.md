What it demonstrates

Juno turns messy PM inputs, such as interview transcripts, support tickets, and emails, into structured insights and an evidence-backed PRD draft.

Debrief
What worked: The three-column flow clearly shows the transformation from raw user input to structured insights and then to a PRD draft.
What broke / felt like a toy: The AI processing is still simulated, and the quality of the generated insights is not yet evaluated against real PM data.
What I'd change next pass: Connect it to a real AI model, test it with real transcripts, and add evaluation criteria and guardrails to measure the quality and reliability of the generated insights.
